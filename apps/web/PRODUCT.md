# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo Thai freelancers running their own business end-to-end — no in-house accountant. They use MANA for projects, contacts, documents, storage, accounting (WHT/VAT/PromptPay), and an AI assistant, often switching between client work and admin within the same session. The tool needs to live alongside their actual work all day, not be a separate destination they dread opening.

## Product Purpose

Give a solo freelancer one place to run the business side of freelancing — projects, contacts, documents, finance/tax (WHT, VAT, PromptPay, ใบกำกับภาษี), and an AI assistant that can act across all of them — without needing an accountant on staff. Success looks like: the user trusts the numbers, finishes admin fast, and keeps using it daily instead of falling back to spreadsheets.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. No mistakes — especially anywhere money or tax filings are involved. Quiet confidence, not flashy persuasion: every screen should read as something that's already correct rather than something trying to convince you it's correct. Comfortable enough to live in all day, every day, in parallel with real client work.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS dashboard cliché (gradient hero metrics, identical icon+text card grids, uppercase eyebrows, hero-metric template).
- Cold enterprise accounting software (QuickBooks/SAP-style density aimed at accountants, not solo freelancers).
- Overly playful consumer app territory (cute illustrations, gamification) — this still handles money and tax, it has to stay serious.

## Design Principles

1. **Be visibly correct, not persuasively correct.** No decoration that implies confidence in place of actually showing the right number, right status, right state.
2. **Optimize for daily residency, not first impressions.** Every surface is judged by "can I work in this for 8 hours," not by how it screenshots once.
3. **Precision over decoration.** Calm neutrals + one functional accent; color and motion are used to communicate state, not to entertain.
4. **Solo-operator scale.** Density and information architecture sized for one person's daily workflow — not an accountant's back office, not a team admin panel.
5. **Admin should feel fast, not heavy.** Forms, tables, and financial views should minimize friction between "client work" and "business admin" context switches.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline across the app: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large/bold text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation. Given the financial/tax stakes, err toward the stricter end of AA rather than the minimum where it's cheap to do so (e.g. status colors, error states).

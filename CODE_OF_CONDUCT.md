# Community code of conduct

MANA welcomes people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient with beginners, and specific when disagreeing. Critique work and ideas without attacking the person. Credit others' contributions and respect privacy.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish personal information or private correspondence without permission. These expectations apply to the project's issues, PRs, reviews, and other official community spaces, including agent-generated contributions.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner, [Duckinm](https://github.com/Duckinm), at [duckii.mt@gmail.com](mailto:duckii.mt@gmail.com). Include relevant links and enough context to investigate; avoid sending unrelated private information. This address was confirmed by the owner for conduct and security reports. There is no guaranteed response time.

The owner may request a correction, remove content, warn a participant, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation according to severity and repetition. Explain moderation decisions to affected people when doing so does not expose private reports or create additional harm. Reports should be shared only as needed to investigate and respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

To appeal a moderation decision, contact the same address with the decision, your concern, and any new evidence. MANA currently has one moderation owner and no independent appeals panel. If a concern involves the owner and you prefer an independent channel, use GitHub's platform reporting tools for conduct on GitHub.
